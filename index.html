01 — My Story

From code to strategy: an engineering journey

The Foundation

My journey began with a Bachelor's in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering — training me in systems thinking and analytical rigor. I quickly discovered that the most complex and exciting "circuits" weren't in hardware labs; they were hidden inside enterprise data.

The Pivot into Data

I dove headfirst into data engineering, joining UBS and BNP Paribas where I built some of my first large-scale pipelines — from scraping Fortune 500 company data with Selenium to streaming SWIFT financial messages with Apache Kafka at millisecond latency for the entire APAC region. I learned that raw data is liability; processed, governed data is an asset.

The Deep Specialization

A Master's in Information Systems at Stevens Institute of Technology gave me both the technical depth and business vocabulary to bridge engineering and executive decision-making — earning Beta Gamma Sigma recognition as a top 5% scholar. Then came Munich Re Specialty, where I've spent 6+ years in a career-defining run — from Data Engineer to Principal Data Engineer.

Where I Stand Today

Today I lead the data engineering strategy for one of the world's largest reinsurance companies. I've personally engineered pipelines that process 14.7 million+ insurance claims records, operated 370-node Hadoop clusters, and modernized data platforms on Snowflake and Databricks. My product owner mindset ensures every technical decision connects to a business outcome. The mission is always the same: turn messy, complex, enterprise data into clean, trustworthy, decision-ready infrastructure.

"Technical depth gets you a working pipeline. Strategic vision gets that pipeline to change the business." — Harsh Kava · Principal Data Engineer, Munich Re

05 — Case Studies

Engineering that moved the needle

Deep dives into four landmark projects — the problem, the approach, and the measurable outcomes.

⚡
Munich Re Specialty · 2020–2023
Real-Time Claims Intelligence Pipeline
Challenge: 14.7M+ unstructured claims records locked in PDFs, images, emails, and ZIP archives — inaccessible to analytics teams. Manual processing was costing weeks per batch.

Solution: Architected an end-to-end real-time pipeline using Apache Kafka for ingestion, Apache Spark for transformation, and MapReduce on a 370-node Hadoop cluster (HDP 2.2.4 / Cloudera) to extract structured text from all formats. Applied Kerberos/LDAP security throughout.

Result: Claims data went from weeks-old batches to real-time analytics — transforming underwriting decision speed.

BNP Paribas · 2016–2017
SWIFT Messaging Real-Time Pipeline, APAC
Challenge: BNP Paribas's APAC E-Reporting was batch-based, causing reporting delays for all regional clients. SWIFT messages (MT94X & MT103) required real-time visibility for compliance and operations.

Solution: Designed and built Kafka producers/consumers and Spark streaming jobs to process SWIFT messages in real time. Built REST services for Kafka topic posting; redesigned the E-Reporting application from scratch for all APAC clients. Led the engineering org from system-centric to data-centric architecture.

Result: Sub-second SWIFT message processing for all APAC clients. A landmark reference architecture for the APAC data engineering organization.

Healthfirst · 2018
HARP Enrollment Analytics — +17% Impact
Challenge: Healthfirst's Health and Recovery Plans (HARP) were significantly under-enrolled. Leadership lacked the data visibility to identify which populations weren't being reached or why.

Solution: Built comprehensive Power BI dashboards analyzing HARP enrollment patterns across demographics, geography, and referral pathways. Used Python and SAS to process millions of member records, assisted actuarial analysts with data engineering needs, and implemented HIPAA-compliant lean data governance processes in Microsoft Visio.

Result: The dashboards surfaced actionable enrollment gaps — directly leading to targeted outreach campaigns and a 17% increase in HARP plan enrollment.
